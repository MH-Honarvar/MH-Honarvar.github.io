The [Karkia Tarabar Asia](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) landing opens on a port photograph. On that photograph sit the title, one line about logistics, and four modes: road, rail, air, and sea. Under them is the line “Your trust, our responsibility,” and two buttons: request a freight quote, and contact.

The page does not wait for a scroll to say what the company does. The four modes are the small cards on the hero. The longer services, the FIATA note, and the quote form sit further down. Someone who only sees the first view still has the subject and the next step.

## One button is filled

The quote button has a solid fill. The contact button beside it is an outline. Both are there, and one of them carries the visual weight. The page is not shouting several invitations at once. That split is the subject of [One next step](/en/blog/one-next-step/).

Language is separate from this layout. Persian, English, and Russian switch with the language control, and the hero stays the hero. The detail is in [Three languages on one page](/en/blog/three-languages-one-page/).

## What the first view leaves out

The route map, the questions, and the Mashhad office address come later. Putting all of that into the hero would lose the title between blocks. The first view only says this is a freight company, by which modes, and where the quote is.

This note does not claim a speed score. What the page itself shows is the title, the four modes, and the quote button, before the service list.
