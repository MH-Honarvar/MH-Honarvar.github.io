Hi! This is the first post on my blog. I wanted a site that you *feel* the moment it opens.

## What is this built with?

- **Astro** for fast, static pages
- **Three.js** and a GLSL shader for the liquid sphere behind the page
- **GSAP** and **Lenis** for motion and smooth scrolling
