In the first view of the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) two links sit side by side: request a freight quote, and contact. The first is a filled button. The second has an outline and quieter type.

The page did not delete the second path. The phone numbers and WhatsApp are further down as well. The difference is the main invitation. An eye that has just reached the hero sees one solid surface, not two equal ones.

## Why the second one stayed

A freight company does not speak only through a form. Someone who wants the office number should not have to reach the bottom of the page to learn that contact is possible. The outline button says that in the hero, without matching the weight of the quote button.

The four transport cards above the buttons say what the company does. The button says what to do. If each card had its own filled button, the page would be six invitations. The cards link to sections. The main action is one.

The layout of that view is in [The first screen](/en/blog/logistics-first-screen/). This note is only the weight of the two buttons.

## What the button does not calculate

The quote form does not produce a live rate. The page itself says so. The filled button means send the shipment details, not that a price is built on the spot. I will not pretend the button does that sum. The button opens the door. The rate is the specialist’s work.
