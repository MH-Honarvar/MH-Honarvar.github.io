On the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) the language control sits in the top bar. Persian is the default. English and Russian replace the copy in the same sections: the title, the hero line, the four mode names, the services, the questions, and the form.

The address does not change. There are not three pages with three routes. Someone who switches language stays on that page, on the same port photograph, with the quote button in the same place.

## What stays fixed

The photograph, the grid of four cards, and the order of the sections do not move with the language. The filled button is still the quote. The outline button is still contact. Language changes the words, not the job of the buttons. The first view is separate, in [The first screen](/en/blog/logistics-first-screen/).

The page direction is right-to-left in Persian. English and Russian read left to right. The menu and the buttons sit with the direction of the page, not in a grid locked to Persian only.

## The limit of this page

This is not a translation essay. All three languages are on the live site and can be read line by line. Where a Russian line titles a card and the Persian explanation sits under it, both belong to that block. The page is not three separate brochures.

If you read only one of the languages, you still get the same company, the same four modes, and the same quote form.
