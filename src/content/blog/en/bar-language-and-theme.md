In the top bar of the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) two small controls sit together: language, and light or dark. The menu is still home, services, and contact. These two buttons are not menu items.

Language switches Persian, English, and Russian and does not change the address. Light and dark only change the surface. Someone who changes the language does not have to hunt for the color mode again, because both stayed in that row.

## Why they are not split apart

If language lived in the footer and color lived in a settings page, each would be its own trip. Here both are reachable before any scroll. The menu moves you to a section. These two change the page itself.

The port photograph and the place of the quote button do not move with either control. That stillness is in [The port photograph stays](/en/blog/the-port-stays/).

The bar does not get crowded. Two buttons, one job each, beside the menu. Not inside it.
