The hero of the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) is a port photograph. Ships, containers, and four cards — road, rail, air, and sea — sit on that photograph. When the language moves from Persian to English or Russian, the photograph does not move. The cards stay in the same grid. The quote button stays where it was.

If each language brought another photograph, the page would be three heroes. This is one hero whose words change.

## What is allowed to change

The title, the line under it, and the names of the four modes. Those are words. A card may grow a little with a longer word, and the row is still that row. Someone who switches language does not have to learn the button’s place again.

The bar that holds the two controls is in [Language and dark mode in one bar](/en/blog/bar-language-and-theme/). This note is only the stillness of the picture.

## Why this is easier to read

The eye learns the place of the action once. If that place jumped with the language, every switch would be a new layout. This page does not charge that cost. The port photograph is the background of all three languages, not the property of one of them.
