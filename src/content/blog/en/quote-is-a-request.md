On the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) the filled button says request a freight quote. Further down, the form takes origin, destination, cargo, and a way to reply. Beside the form the page says this step does not calculate a price. The details go to the traffic desk, and a specialist gives the rate.

The button does not promise a number on the spot. It promises that the request reaches someone who knows the rate.

## What the form holds

Origin and destination come from port and customs names. Cargo is general, reefer, or dangerous. Dimensions are there for volume. Then a name and a number. That is enough for a request. It is not enough for a contract, and the page does not hide that.

The weight of the two hero buttons is written separately, in [One next step](/en/blog/one-next-step/). This note is only the limit of the form.

## Why this is the more honest door

A calculator that invents a number, and a specialist who then rejects it, spends trust. This form says from the start that the number comes later. Someone who fills it in knows what they sent and what they did not.
