At the bottom of the [Karkia Tarabar Asia landing](https://karkiatarabar.com/landing/) three places are written out. The Mashhad headquarters in the Armitaj Golshan tower, with floor, unit, and postal code. The research office in the faculty at Ferdowsi University. The Bandar Abbas branch in the Safir complex, with floor and unit.

Each one has a map link. This is not decoration at the end of the page. Someone who wants to know where the company is gets an address they can say, not an unnamed pin.

## A number beside the address

The headquarters phone, the research-office phone, and two mobiles for WhatsApp sit in that same section. So does the traffic desk email. An address without a number is half a card. Here each office has its own way in.

The quote form does not replace these numbers. Someone who will not fill in a form can still call. The limit of the form is in [The form is a request, not a rate](/en/blog/quote-is-a-request/).

## Three languages, the same address

Persian, English, and Russian name the same street and the same postal code. Language changes the words, not the place. If only one of the three blocks were translated, the page would hide an office. All three blocks exist in all three languages.
